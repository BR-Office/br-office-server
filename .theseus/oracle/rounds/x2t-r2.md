## Reading
- Empty-template → PDF is still 0.68–1.25 s against ~0.12 s for every other direction: the fixed JS cost
  (script load + first Execute) remains the dominant x2t cost a user feels, after snapshots (PKT-0065: −0.1 s).
- The direct PDF numbers for 10-page/500-row/12-slide inputs (5–6.5 s) are under VM contention with the parallel
  browser runs of other worktrees; PKT-0041/0051 measured 1.3 s for docx 10 p alone. Not comparable to baseline.
- In the browser, x2t's part (save 0.2–0.65 s, convert 0.2–0.8 s) is small next to open/join (3.3–7.6 s, client).
- No speed or quality change in x2t can be attributed to round 1: x2t binary is unchanged.

## Run 2 (same day, same binary): PASS

same-binary noise: a flake, not a difference. Direct pdf medians this run (ms, orig/new): new.docx 1710/1735,
new.xlsx 1065/1011, new.pptx 1130/1353, text-10p.docx 3068/2801, rows-500.xlsx 2274/2748, slides-12.pptx 2808/2944

is the part that scales with contention (CPU-bound JS warm-up).
