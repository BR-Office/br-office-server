## Problem
AUD-6's 21 records include 8 human discards never run (PKT-0117 … 0124), so the audit triggered on 13 runs and nearly every statement fell under the 20-record observation rule.

## Proposed change
In method-measurement §5 and the planner: "`audit.every_n_packets` counts records with an executor; records discarded before a claim are reported in the audit's sample line but not counted."

## Hypothesis
"The next audit has at least 20 run records, so at least 2 of its TEP verdicts reach the 20-record minimum, with no increase in the time between audits beyond 1.5× the AUD-5→AUD-6 interval."

## How to verify
AUD-7 sample line: run records ≥20; count verdicts with sample ≥20.

## Risks
Mass discards (a plan reversal) go unaudited longer; the sample line still lists them.
