## Problem
7 of 22 records were rejected on R2 for files in `external/sdkjs` (AUD-5 O1): diffs taken against origin/main instead of the fork base (PKT-0085, 0087, 0058, 0090 — each blocked after 3 identical rejections, $5.31) and a `.theseus-addons` gitlink created by the oracle and auto-committed by the factory (PKT-0087, 0091, 0095, 0093). Executors could not run the check before delivery (PKT-0095 friction). `external/sdkjs.base` was cloned by hand in PKT-0097, 0098.

## Proposed change
In the factory spec: "The R2 check of a nested clone diffs it against the commit the clone was provisioned at. Paths listed in `adapter.scope.ignore` (default `.theseus-addons/`) are excluded from both the auto-commit and the check. `theseus scope-check` runs the same check for the executor. The factory provisions every `*.base` clone the oracle declares. A gate rejection identical to the previous attempt's blocks the packet (extends TEP-0009 to gate rejections)."

## Hypothesis
"R2 rejections on nested clones fall from 7 of 22 records (AUD-5) to at most 1 in the next 20, and blocked cost falls from 22% to under 10% of period spend, with no file outside a target reaching done."

## How to verify
AUD-6 counts R2 rejection reasons naming `external/`, blocked cost share, and post-merge diffs of done packets. Minimum sample 20 records.

## Risks
An over-broad ignore list could hide real out-of-target edits; watch escapes with `failed_component: review`.
