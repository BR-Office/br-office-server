## Problem
88 permission denials in 21 of 27 records of AUD-2; 22 of 45 friction lines are factory permission dumps, and agents repeat the complaint in PKT-0030, 0031, 0032, 0033, 0037, 0038, 0039, 0049. Most denied commands only read: `sed -n`, `head`, `ls; cat`, `find -exec wc`, pipes into `grep`/`wc`. Same signal as AUD-1 O6.

## Proposed change
In the adapter's factory permissions, add an allowlist of read-only commands that is accepted also when chained with `;`, `&&` or `|` and inside `cd <worktree>`: `cat, head, tail, sed -n, grep, ls, wc, find (without -delete/-exec other than wc/grep/head), git log/show/diff/status, python3 -I reading files`. Commands that write stay under approval.

## Hypothesis
"With the read-only allowlist, permission denials per record fall by at least 70% (from 3.3 to ≤1.0), with no increase in contract violations or files changed outside the target."

## How to verify
Count `permission_denials` per record and `contract_violated` rejections in AUD-3. Minimum sample: 20 records.

## Risks
A pattern that looks read-only but writes (e.g. `sed -i`, `find -delete`, redirection `>`). Watch contract violations and out-of-target diffs.

## Decision
Rafael, 2026-11-03: rejected as superseded. He had already granted agents every privilege in this disposable VM, which took permission denials from 3.3 per record to 0 (AUD-3 F2); a read-only allowlist adds nothing here.
