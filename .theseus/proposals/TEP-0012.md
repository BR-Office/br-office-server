## Problem
`theseus packet new` offers no type for comment/doc/environment work or for re-measurements: PKT-0090 and PKT-0097 filed chores as `refactor`, PKT-0091 filed a browser re-measure as `characterize` (friction). Routing by type is skewed.

## Proposed change
In work-packet spec: add `chore` (no behaviour change; acceptance may be the module's fast oracle only) and `measure` (produces numbers into an Atlas/baseline file; acceptance must check the number, not only its presence).

## Hypothesis
"Friction lines about a missing packet type fall from 3 (AUD-5) to 0 in the next 20 records, and median acceptance time of comment-only packets falls below 1 minute, with first-review approval of the new types ≥ 75%."

## How to verify
AUD-6 counts type friction, acceptance duration of chore packets, approval per type. Minimum sample: 3 packets of each new type, else observation.

## Risks
Types used to dodge stronger acceptance; watch escapes on chore packets.

Rafael, 2026-11-03: approved. Applied in Theseus 0.22.0; method version 0.5.0.
