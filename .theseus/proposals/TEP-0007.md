## Problem
Packets reach the queue unusable or under-scoped. Placeholders: PKT-0053, PKT-0055 (3 attempts on `[ORACLE COMMAND]`), discarded PKT-0060, 0061, 0062; AUD-2 O5 (PKT-0021, 0022). Goal outside target: PKT-0057, 0063, 0064 blocked; PKT-0041, 0051, 0046 delivered partially and spawned follow-ups.

## Proposed change
Add to the work-packet specification: "`theseus validate` and `theseus claim` refuse a packet whose target, contract or acceptance contains a `[UPPERCASE]` placeholder, or whose acceptance list is empty for types other than `investigate`; and warn when a repository path named in the Goal is outside `target.files` and `target.create_in`."

## Hypothesis
"With the issue-time lint, blocked + discarded records caused by placeholders or Goal paths outside target fall from 11 of 27 records (AUD-3) to at most 3 of the next 27, without lowering done packets per period."

## How to verify
Count blocked/discarded records whose reason names a placeholder or a file outside target in AUD-4. Minimum sample: 20 records.

## Risks
False warnings on Goal paths mentioned only as context; watch time to issue and the share of packets the planner overrides.
