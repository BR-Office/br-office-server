## Problem
Work was lost between attempts in PKT-0068, 0066, 0078, 0071 and 0065 (AUD-4 O3): diffs missing after restart, commits in external/sdkjs reset, packets citing rebased SHAs. PKT-0078 took 4 attempts and 41 minutes.

## Proposed change
In the factory spec: "Before each new attempt the factory commits the worktree and each nested clone under `target` to `attempt/<id>/<n>` branches and starts the next attempt from them; packets reference branches, never bare SHAs."

## Hypothesis
"Friction lines reporting lost attempt work fall from 5 packets (AUD-4) to at most 1 in the next 20 records, and median duration of multi-attempt done packets falls by 30%, with no increase in R2 rejections."

## How to verify
AUD-5 counts friction texts on lost/reset/rebased work and multi-attempt durations. Minimum sample 20 records.

## Risks
Preserved attempts may carry a broken approach forward; watch rejections on attempt ≥2 repeating attempt-1 reasons.
