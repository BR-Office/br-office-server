## Problem
All 7 blocked records in AUD-1 (PKT-0002 x2, PKT-0004, PKT-0011, PKT-0013, PKT-0009 x2) were caused by tooling or rules (friction F5, F6, F8, F12, F13, F16), yet they count against the executor model. Five of them are Sonnet records, which makes Sonnet on characterize packets look like 1 of 6 approved at first review. Blocked attempts cost $13.92, 40% of total spend.

## Proposed change
`block_reason` becomes `{"cause": "method" | "model" | "task", "ref": "<rule or spec section>", "text": "..."}`. The integrator or reviewer sets it whenever a packet is blocked. Routing statistics (the Routing row of §4.4) exclude records with `cause: method`, and the audit reports the cost of method-caused blocks as a separate indicator.

## Hypothesis
Once blocks are attributed, method-caused block cost falls below 15% of total spend within the next 20 packets, because it becomes visible and can trigger an alarm. Per-model approval rates change by no more than noise once method-caused blocks are excluded.

## How to verify
In AUD-2, measure the share of blocked records that have a `cause`, the method-caused block cost divided by total cost, and the first-review rate per model with and without method-caused blocks (minimum 20 packets).

## Risks
Blocks may be attributed to "method" to protect a model's numbers. The auditor samples run folders to check.
