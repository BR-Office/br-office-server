## Problem
The fields `estimated`, `context_ratio`, `out_of_context_reads` and `flaky_oracle` are null in 22/22 records. As a result, the Packet and Atlas indicators and part of the Oracle indicators in §4.4 cannot be computed.

Friction is also thin:
- 12 of the 15 friction lines are factory permission dumps.
- 7 of the 10 done packets have no friction line written by the agent: PKT-0001, 0003, 0004, 0005, 0013, 0014 and 0015 (AUD-1 O6, O7).

## Proposed change
1. The planner writes `estimated: {files, diff_lines}` on every packet it creates.
2. The factory records `context_ratio` and `out_of_context_reads` from the agent run. Reads of files not listed in `context` count as out-of-context reads.
3. `theseus move <id> review` fails unless `friction.jsonl` has a line for the packet without `source: factory`.

## Hypothesis
"With these changes, AUD-2 can compute all 7 indicator rows of §4.4. At least 90% of done packets have an agent friction line, up from 30%, and the time from claim to review grows by no more than 5%."

## How to verify
In AUD-2, count non-null indicator fields and the share of done packets with an agent friction line. Minimum sample: 20 records.

## Risks
Agents may write boilerplate friction lines. To detect this, count duplicate or near-duplicate texts across packets.
