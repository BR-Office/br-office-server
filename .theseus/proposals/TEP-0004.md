## Problem
PKT-0010 and PKT-0012 were approved with an empty executor log (F15). PKT-0009 was approved without the follow-up it was told to spawn (F18, F19). PKT-0002 was approved after a surprising behavior was dropped from its tests (F7).

## Proposed change
Add to the reviewer prompt: "Reject the packet if the Executor log is empty; if the packet asked for follow-ups and neither Spawned packets nor `.theseus/spawn/` lists them; or, for characterize packets, if a surprising behavior mentioned in the log is not kept in a test."

## Hypothesis
In the next 20 done packets, none is approved with an empty executor log and no requested follow-up is missing at approval. First-review approval stays at or above 60% (currently 70%).

## How to verify
In AUD-2, scan done packets for empty logs and compare requested with spawned follow-ups. Take the first-review rate from packets.jsonl.

## Risks
More rejections for missing paperwork. The first-review rate, its balancing indicator, will show this.
