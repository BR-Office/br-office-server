## Problem
Friction lines F1–F11 follow §4.3 (`role`, `model`, and `hindered`/`missing`/`unneeded` with a `ref`). Lines F12–F21 use `phase`, `kind` and `note`, with no `ref`, `role` or `model`. Half of the period's friction cannot be counted per rule.

## Proposed change
§4.3: "Every friction line has `packet`, `role`, `model`, `date` and at least one of `hindered`, `missing`, `unneeded` or `helped`, each an object with `ref` and `text`. `theseus validate` rejects any other shape."

## Hypothesis
100% of friction lines written after the change pass validation, and at least 80% carry a `ref` that resolves to a spec or adapter section. Friction lines per finished packet do not drop (currently 21 lines for 21 packets).

## How to verify
Run `theseus validate` on friction.jsonl, and count lines per packet in AUD-2 (minimum 20 packets).

## Risks
A stricter format may discourage reporting. Watch the number of lines per packet.
