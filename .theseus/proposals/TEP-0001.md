## Problem
In PKT-0002, PKT-0016 (two records) and PKT-0017, every rejection repeated the same blocker, and the reviewer named a remedy only a human or the factory could apply. Examples: "factory must add INDEX.md to the target", "allow gh api", "an admin must fix the PostgreSQL install". Attempts 2 and 3 added no information. Records that ended blocked cost $7.34, which is 38% of the period's $19.44 (AUD-1 O2).

## Proposed change
In the review spec, add a required `blocker` field to every rejection: `executor` (the executor can fix it) or `external` (target, permission, environment or goal wording). When the factory sees `blocker: external`, it moves the packet to `blocked` at once with `block_reason.cause` set to `method` or `environment`. It does not start another attempt.

## Hypothesis
"Blocking at once on external rejections cuts the cost of blocked records per blocked packet by at least 50%, while the first-review approval rate and escapes stay the same."

## How to verify
Compare the cost per blocked packet in the next audit with $1.84 (AUD-1: $7.34 / 4 costed blocked records). Check that 0 packets labelled `external` were later fixed by the executor alone. Minimum sample: 5 blocked packets.

## Risks
Reviewers may over-label rejections as `external` to skip work, which would raise the blocked count. To detect this, count blocked packets that a rerun later finished with no change to the target, permissions or environment.
