## Problem
All 28 records in AUD-1 lack `estimated`, `context_ratio`, `out_of_context_reads`, `spawned_packets`, `rejection_reasons`, `block_reason` and `flaky_oracle`, so 4 of the 7 indicators in §4.4 cannot be computed. Every record says `method_version: 0.1.0`, while friction lines F8, F12, F13, F16 and F19 cite tool fixes in Theseus 0.7.1–0.9.0. As a result, no fix can be compared before and after.

## Proposed change
In §4.1 add: "The factory writes every field of this section on every record; a missing value is written as `null`, never omitted. Each record also carries `tool_version` (the Theseus CLI version that ran the packet)." In §3 add: "A tooling change that alters packet flow bumps the method patch version."

## Hypothesis
After the change, at least 95% of new records contain all §4.1 keys and `tool_version`, and AUD-2 can compute at least 6 of the 7 indicators in §4.4. Median factory overhead per packet does not rise by more than 5%.

## How to verify
Count keys in records written after the version bump (minimum 20 records). Count computable indicators in AUD-2.

## Risks
Agents may guess values for `out_of_context_reads` and `context_ratio`. Check their variance and spot-check them against the run folders.
