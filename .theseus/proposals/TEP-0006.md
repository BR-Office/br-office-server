## Problem
TEP-0001 to 0004 are `testing` (implemented in Theseus 0.14.0), but all 27 records of AUD-2 report `method_version: 0.1.0` and METHOD-CHANGELOG lists only 0.1.0; AUD-2 had to split before/after by `tool_version`. Also `estimated.source` is `budget` in 23/23 filled records, with actual diff a median 16% of it, so the "estimated vs actual" indicator measures budget slack, not slicing.

## Proposed change
1. In §6: "When a TEP moves to `testing`, the method version is bumped and METHOD-CHANGELOG gets an entry listing the TEP and its hypothesis; the factory writes that version on every record."
2. In the work packet spec: the planner writes `estimated: {files, diff_lines}` on every packet; `source: budget` is allowed only for hand-written packets and the audit excludes those from the deviation indicator.

## Hypothesis
"In AUD-3, 100% of records carry a method_version that maps to a changelog entry, and ≥80% of records have a planner estimate, with the median |actual/estimated - 1| below 1.0; planning cost per packet grows by no more than 10%."

## How to verify
Count records whose `method_version` appears in METHOD-CHANGELOG; count `estimated.source != budget`; compute the deviation. Minimum sample: 20 records.

## Risks
Estimates written by habit (always the same number). Watch the spread of estimates across packets.
