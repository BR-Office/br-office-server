## Problem
PKT-0080 shipped acceptance `node .theseus/oracle/perf/profile-fonts.js <samples-dir> external/sdkjs 3`; it failed 3 times with the same output, PKT-0081 copied it, and the fix packets PKT-0083 and PKT-0086 blocked too: 4 blocked records, $4.36 (AUD-4 O1). TEP-0007 lints only `[UPPERCASE]`. Spawn requests reached review with placeholders 5 times (PKT-0068, 0069, 0075, 0080, 0083).

## Proposed change
In work-packet R6: "`theseus validate`, `claim` and `packet new` (on save of a spawn request) refuse a packet or request whose target, contract or acceptance contains `[UPPERCASE]`, `<lowercase-word>` or `TODO` placeholders. When an acceptance command fails twice with identical output, the factory blocks the packet at once with `cause: method, ref: oracle#deterministic` instead of a third attempt."

## Hypothesis
"Blocked records whose reason is a placeholder or a repeated identical acceptance failure fall from 4 of 20 (AUD-4) to at most 1 of the next 20, and R6 rejections of spawn requests from 5 to at most 1, with no drop in done packets."

## How to verify
AUD-5 counts block_reason / rejection_reasons naming placeholders or identical output. Minimum sample 20 records.

## Risks
A legitimate flaky failure that repeats identically gets blocked early; watch blocked records later resolved by a plain rerun.
