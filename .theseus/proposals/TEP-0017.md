## Problem
PKT-0109 and PKT-0149 were rejected on a differential failure whose rejection text quotes only the summary line; both report the difference does not reproduce on an unchanged fork. PKT-0133 blocked on "1 unintended difference" after 3 attempts. `flaky_oracle` is false on 24/24 recorded rows of AUD-7 and on every row of AUD-5/6, while friction reports noise.

## Proposed change
Oracle spec: "On failure the differential prints each differing case and check id. It reruns only the differing checks once; a check that passes on rerun is reported as flaky, does not fail acceptance, and sets `flaky_oracle: true` on the record."

## Hypothesis
"In the next 20 records, rejections citing a differential summary without a case id fall from 3 (AUD-7) to 0, and at least one record has `flaky_oracle: true`, with no escape tagged `failed_component: oracle`."

## How to verify
Scan `rejection_reasons` for differential failures without case ids; count `flaky_oracle` true; escapes.jsonl. Minimum 3 differential runs that fail.

## Risks
A real intermittent regression could be passed as flaky; escapes with `failed_component: oracle` will show it. Rerun adds minutes to failing runs only.
